What kind of game is this?

Christmas Pool: Holiday Shots is a level-based pool game. Aim the cue, control shot power, pocket the object balls and work through varied table layouts.

Does the game contain advertising?

Yes. The game may show interstitial and optional rewarded advertising. Rewarded ads are presented as a choice when available.

Are rewards guaranteed?

No. In-game reward opportunities, availability and eligibility may change. They are not employment or guaranteed income. Please read the Terms of Service for details.

How is information handled?

The game uses advertising, attribution, analytics and notification providers. Our Privacy Policy describes the data categories, purposes and choices.
