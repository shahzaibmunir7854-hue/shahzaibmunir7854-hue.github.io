Before you begin

A few sweet details.

How do I make a match?

Tap available goods to add them to the collection tray. Gather three of the same type to clear them. Keep an eye on the remaining tray spaces.

What can the tools do?

The game includes tools for rearranging goods, finding a match, and moving items. Availability and any requirements are shown in the game.

Does the game include advertising?

Yes. The game integrates advertising services. For details about associated data use and privacy choices, read our Privacy Policy.
