Playing the game

Tap available goods to add them to the tray. Three matching goods clear together. Plan around the available slots, and check the in-game tool instructions when you need help.

Technical issues

If the app becomes unresponsive, close and reopen it. Check your device’s storage and connection, and keep the app updated. Clearing app data or reinstalling can erase locally stored progress.
